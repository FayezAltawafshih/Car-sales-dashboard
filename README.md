# 🚗 Car Sales Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that turns **23,906 car sales records (2022–2023)** into clear, actionable insights.

![Dashboard preview](images/dashboard.png)

## 📌 Overview
The dataset covers **30 car brands**, **28 dealerships**, and **7 regions**. The goal is to understand what drives sales and present the findings in an easy-to-explore dashboard.

## 📊 Key Metrics
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $671.5M |
| Total Cars Sold | 23,906 |
| Average Car Price | ≈ $28,090 |
| Average Customer Income | ≈ $830,840 |

## 🛠️ Workflow
1. **Data cleaning (Power Query):** imported the data, fixed encoding issues (e.g., in engine type values), and converted it into an Excel Table.
2. **Pivot Tables (11):** top brands by sales value, cars sold per brand, body style, transmission, engine type, models, colors, top dealers, and monthly trends.
3. **KPIs:** total sales, cars sold, average price, and average customer income.
4. **Dashboard:** 9 charts combined in one clean layout.
5. **Interactivity:** Slicers for **Dealer**, **Company**, and **Model** update all charts and KPIs at once.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Chevrolet, Ford, and Dodge lead in total sales value.
- Hatchbacks and SUVs are the most popular body styles.
- Sales rise in the second half of the year, peaking in September, November, and December.
- Automatic (12,571) and manual (11,335) transmissions sell in nearly equal numbers.

## ▶️ How to Use
1. Download `Car_Data_Project.xlsx` and open it in Microsoft Excel (desktop version recommended for Slicers and Power Query).
2. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet and use the Slicers to filter by dealer, company, or model.
